---
name: Subtitle Canon Research
description: Research series-wide and title-specific names, terminology, gadgets, places, organizations, and translation history before subtitle editing.
---

Separate stable project canon from title-only terminology.

Evidence preference: official/localized releases and first-party materials first; reliable databases/reference works second; community material only for gaps or disputed usage.

For every proposed term record canonical target, aliases, whether replacement is deterministic, whether context is required, branch scope, notes, and evidence. Never make a context-sensitive alias auto-replaceable.
